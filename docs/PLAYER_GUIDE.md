# Nexora SkyBlock player guide (early test build)

When access is opened, new players arrive at the NEXORA Hub. `/hub` returns to it. `/island` and `/is` are provided by BentoBox/BSkyBlock; use `/island create` to create an island. The compass in hotbar slot 9 opens the main NEXORA menu. It includes Profile, Progression, Skills, Collections, Minions, Daily Tasks and warps to the Hub, private island, Mining Realm and Farming Realm. `/profile`, `/progress`, `/skills`, `/collections`, and `/daily` are direct shortcuts; `/coins` prints your wallet balance. SkyBlock XP, skill XP, collection milestones and daily quest claims are saved per player. Craft a minion using eight of its output resource around an Armor Stand, place it on an island where BentoBox allows building, then right-click to collect or upgrade. Sneak-right-click an empty minion to pick it up. `/minion list` shows your placed workers.

This is a restricted development server and whitelist is enabled. Bazaar and Auction House are not available yet. Minions do not yet have fuel, compactors or automated selling; offline production is capped at 24 hours and storage capacity.

Survival rules: food never decreases. On death, your inventory and experience are kept. Half of the Coins in your purse are lost, except when you die on an island where you are the owner or a team member. Tools and weapons do not lose durability.
